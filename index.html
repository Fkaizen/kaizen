Calculator guideQuick steps and key reference
01

Enter and calculate

Type or tap an expression to see its live preview. Press Enter or = to save it to history. Use parentheses to group parts, like (2+3)*4.

02

Use scientific functions

Functions take a value in parentheses: sin(30), sqrt(81), log(100), ln(e), or abs(-4). Inverse trig functions are asin, acos, and atan.

Choose DEG or RAD for trig angle units. In DEG mode, sin(30) equals 0.5.

03

Operators and constants

+ add · - subtract · * multiply · / divide · % remainder · ^ power · ! factorial.

Use pi, e, and Ans for constants and your last result. Write multiplication explicitly, for example 2*pi.

04

Browse formulas and history

Search or filter the formula library, then choose Try example to load it. Select a history item to restore an expression; press Clear to remove the list.
